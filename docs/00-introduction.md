# Welcome to Big Data & Connectionist AI

This learning material introduces two important areas of modern technology: **Big Data and Connectionist AI**. You will explore how large amounts of data are collected, stored, filtered, and analyzed, and how artificial neural networks learn from data to make predictions and solve problems.

To be more specific:
Part 1: Big Data
In this part, we explore how large amounts of data are collected, stored, processed, and analyzed. You will learn about the different types of data, the challenges encountered in working with it, and how data filtration and analysis help turn raw data into useful information.

Part 2: Connectionist AI
In this part, we introduce Connectionist AI and the artificial neural networks behind it. You will discover how artificial neurons work, how neural networks are structured, and how they learn from data through training, optimization, and evaluation.

Part 3: A couple words on applications/ implementations.

**By connecting these three parts, you will build an understanding of how data becomes meaningful information and how AI uses data to learn and make predictions.**

Our goal is to make these complex topics understandable and accessible, even for beginners. Through clear explanations, key concepts, and practical applications, we hope to help you understand not only how these technologies work, but also how they are used in the real world.

As the creators of this material, we hope to make your learning journey clear, engaging, and rewarding. By the end, we wish you to have a solid foundation in Big Data and Connectionist AI, and a better understanding of how they work together to shape modern technology.

***We wish you an enjoyable learning experience!***
