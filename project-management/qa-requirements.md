## 2. Rule handling

- R01 says to use current assignment instructions and attachments; explicit specific requirements override shared rules where an exception is stated.
- R08 explicitly removes the compulsory Lexue platform task from last year. It still requires meaningful activities and permits appropriate links for external resources and optional supplements, with core content, instructions, and expected outcomes in the submission.
- R09 explicitly requires editable PPTX and presenter notes. R15's older PDF-version wording does not make PDF-only submission sufficient.
- R08 explicitly requires explained answer keys. R19's generic statement that explanations are optional does not remove that requirement.
- R10 and R21 specify 1–8 MP4 videos, each at most 250 MB. The 5–10 minute suggestion is not a fixed duration limit.
- Checklist completion is evidence of checks performed, not a guarantee of a grade. QA severity labels and internal approval decisions are team procedures, not teacher scoring rules.
- Do not silently replace conflicting deadlines, rubrics, or peer-review scope. Record the teacher's clarification and its date.

## 5. Rubric selection

### Specific Stage 2 Video V1 rubric — R21

| Criterion | Maximum points |
|---|---:|
| Essential coverage and tutorial structure | 20 |
| Accuracy and explanatory quality | 20 |
| Audiovisual design and technical quality | 20 |
| Engagement, creativity, and pacing | 15 |
| Active learning and demonstrations | 15 |
| Coherence across the learning system | 10 |
| Total | 100 |

R21 performance bands are Outstanding 90–100%, Excellent 75–89%, Good 60–74%, Fair 40–59%, Poor 1–39%, and missing/no assessable evidence 0% of each criterion's available points. The total is rounded to the nearest whole point. Judge the complete final compressed collection and avoid duplicate penalties for the same shortcoming. A course-XP conversion is not specified here.

| Worksheet | Listed weights | Use |
